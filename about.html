Event Details

Format: Online Competition

🎯 The Challenge

Codeception 2025 presents a unique web development puzzle where participants explore a website within a website. You will navigate through multiple layers of web pages, each containing hidden clues embedded in the source code, styling, and interactive elements.

Every detail matters—from HTML comments to CSS properties, JavaScript functions to network requests. The faster you decode the mysteries, the higher your score.

📋 Rules & Format

01

Individual Competition

This is an individual event. Each participant competes independently.

02

Registration Required

All participants must register before the event starts. No late entries will be accepted.

03

Time Limit

You have exactly 2 hours to solve as many puzzles as possible.

04

Scoring System

Points are awarded based on puzzle difficulty and time taken. Speed matters!

05

Tools Allowed

Browser DevTools, text editors, and online documentation are permitted. No automated bots.

06

Fair Play

Communication with other participants during the competition is strictly prohibited.

🔍 How It Works

  1. Access the Puzzle: After registration, participants will receive a unique code to access the main puzzle website at the event start time.
  2. Explore the Code: Use your browser's developer tools to inspect HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and network activity.
  3. Find Hidden Clues: Clues may be hidden in comments, encoded in scripts, disguised in styling, or revealed through interactions.
  4. Solve Puzzles: Each clue leads to the next layer. Decode patterns, solve logic puzzles, and uncover secret paths.
  5. Submit Answers: Input your solutions through the puzzle interface. Correct answers unlock new challenges.
  6. Race the Clock: Your final score depends on both accuracy and speed. The leaderboard updates in real-time.

Top-performing participants will be recognized on our leaderboard and receive certificates of achievement. Special prizes await the winners!
