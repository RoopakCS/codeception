About the Event

The Challenge

In Codeception, you’ll explore a website packed with hidden clues buried in code, styles, and console logs. Use your curiosity, logic, and web dev skills to uncover the final secret flag.

Every detail matters—from HTML comments to CSS properties, JavaScript functions to network requests. The faster you decode the mysteries, the higher your score.

Rules & Format

01

Individual Competition

This is an individual event. Each participant competes independently.

02

Registration Required

All participants must register before the event starts. No late entries will be accepted.

03

Time Limit

You have exactly 2 hours to solve as many puzzles as possible.

04

Scoring System

Points are awarded based on puzzle difficulty and time taken. Speed matters!

05

Tools Allowed

Browser DevTools, text editors, and online documentation are permitted. No automated bots.

06

Fair Play

Communication with other participants during the competition is strictly prohibited.

How It Works

  1. Register & Begin: Participants register for the event and receive access to the official Codeception website once the challenge starts.
  2. Inspect & Discover: Use your browser’s Developer Tools to dig through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and console logs. Every hint is hidden somewhere in the site.
  3. Follow the Clues: Each discovery leads you closer to the next step. Think logically, decode patterns, and explore every corner of the website.
  4. Find the Answers: Use your web development skills to solve the puzzles and uncover secret messages or links.
  5. Reach the Final Goal: All your clues will lead to the ultimate hidden flag. The first to find and submit it wins!
